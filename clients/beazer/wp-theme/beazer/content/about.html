01 The Name

A name is just a name

Beazer is a rock band from Trinidad and Tobago, built around the guitarist and songwriter whose surname it carries.

The name was not a branding exercise. Every other name they reached for was already taken, and if they had carried on they would just have got lawsuits. So a close friend suggested the simplest option left: be yourself. Seeing his own surname on a flyer took some getting used to.

His answer to anyone who thinks it is an odd name for a rock band is short. A name is just a name. Korn became one of the biggest bands in the world.

The line-up did not come together through auditions either. These are players who have been crossing each other’s paths for decades on a small island scene, in Orange Sky, in Jointpop and in bands in Europe, and who finally had the same year free at the same time. Joel calls it destiny, then apologises for how that sounds.

What they are making is rock, played by people who grew up on it in a country built on soca and calypso. That tension is the point rather than a problem to be solved: the band drifted away from their elders’ music because it did not sound ready to travel, and then found they had carried the island with them anyway.

It’s quite melodic, with heavy undertones, with punk attitude.

02 The Band

The line-up

01

Joel Beazer

Guitar · Vocals

Guitarist and songwriter, and the reason the band is called what it is. Grandson and son of legendary Trinidadian calypso artists: by his own account he was supposed to be singing more calypso than anybody, and went to rock instead. Spent years playing behind other bands in Europe before putting his own name on a flyer.

02

Obasi Springer

Drums

Known around Trinidad as the drum god. Played alongside Joel in Orange Sky. He is the one who says the island is in the music whether the band admits it or not: “we drinking the water here in this island, we smelling the Sahara dust.”

03

Jerome

Bass

Held down the bass in Jointpop, one of Trinidad’s best known rock bands, for years. He and Joel met as teenagers and had been circling a project together ever since. Joel’s summary is short: a solid, solid bass player, and every band needs one.

03 The Team

The people behind it

Joey Sabeeney

Manager · Titan Music

He walked into Eclipse Studio to listen to a pair of speakers, knowing nothing about the music industry, and walked out managing Orange Sky. He stayed fourteen years: over four hundred shows, half of them outside Trinidad, a US tour, dates in England and as far as India, and three independent record deals.

He has spent the years since promoting, and says he did not expect to manage a band again.

I don’t see anybody else knocking on Joel’s door. Maybe they’re not hearing or seeing what I am.

Orange Sky’s first manager and the producer of their first album, long before any of this. He saw Joel play at Buckfest and then spent months telling Titan Music to do something about it. He is financing the recordings and will share the international side of the management.
