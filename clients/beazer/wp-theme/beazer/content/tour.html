02 On stage · 3 October

The room

It looks like a castle from the road, and there is a whole universe inside. The band rate the stage as one of the more impressive they have played on at home.

The light

Executive producer Bruce Bressler is bringing a lighting design cued to the music, which is not how a local rock show usually gets put on.

The stakes

The night raises funds toward a festival the band has been invited to play. Producer Jeff Glicksman and Bressler are both flying in for it.

If you’re looking to see something authentic, come to Hutt Shutts.
